## 1. Overview

Kine is a media player application for Meta Quest devices. It is designed to let you play, organize, and access media from sources you choose, such as files on your device, local network shares, media servers, streams, and optional add-ons.

Kine is designed to operate primarily on your device. We do not operate user accounts for Kine, we do not sell personal information, we do not use the Meta Advertising ID, and we do not use your media library for advertising or profiling.

This Privacy Policy explains what data Kine processes, where that data is stored, how it is used, and how you can delete it.

## 2. Data Kine processes on your device

Kine may process and store the following information locally on your Meta Quest device, depending on the features you use:

| Type of data | Examples | Purpose | Where it is stored |
|---|---|---|---|
| Media library data | File names, folder names, media titles, album/artist information, video titles, thumbnails, artwork, subtitles, playlists, library database entries | To display, organize, search, and play your media | Locally in Kine’s app data on your device |
| Playback data | Watch history, resume points, playback progress, recently played items | To resume playback and improve navigation inside the app | Locally in Kine’s app data on your device |
| App settings | Display settings, audio settings, language settings, interface preferences, library configuration, add-on settings | To remember your preferences | Locally in Kine’s app data on your device |
| Media source information | Local folders, network paths, server addresses, URLs, SMB/NFS/WebDAV/UPnP/DLNA sources, streaming sources | To connect to media sources you configure | Locally in Kine’s app data on your device |
| Saved credentials, if you choose to save them | Usernames, passwords, tokens, or keys for network shares, media servers, or third-party services | To authenticate to services or devices you configure | Locally in Kine’s app data on your device; sent only to the service you selected when needed for authentication |
| Local logs | App version, errors, add-on names, source paths, URLs, device/app configuration, and diagnostic messages | To help diagnose problems | Locally on your device unless you choose to send them to us |
| Device interaction data | Headset orientation, controller input, button input, and session/display state used by the app | To render the interface and respond to user input | Processed during use; not sent to us |
| Meta Store / entitlement status | App entitlement or launch status, where required by Meta Store distribution | To verify authorized use and run the app | Processed through Meta platform services; we do not receive your Meta login credentials |

Kine does **not** intentionally collect or send us your media files, media library, playback history, saved passwords, local file paths, network-share credentials, controller tracking data, headset pose data, or Meta account credentials.

## 3. Data Kine does not collect

Unless this policy is updated to say otherwise, Kine does not collect or use:

- Advertising IDs.
- Precise location or approximate location.
- Camera images or passthrough camera images.
- Microphone audio.
- Eye tracking, face tracking, or body tracking data.
- Meta friends lists, social graph data, profile photos, or contacts.
- Payment card details.
- Health information.
- Automatically uploaded analytics from the app to our own servers.
- Automatically uploaded crash logs from the app to our own servers.

## 4. Data you may send to us voluntarily

You may choose to contact us for support, bug reports, privacy requests, or security reports. If you do, we may receive the information you choose to provide, such as:

- Your name or email address.
- Your message.
- App version and device model.
- Screenshots, videos, or logs you attach.
- Error details or reproduction steps.
- Any media paths, URLs, add-on names, or configuration details included in logs you choose to send.

We use this information only to respond to your request, troubleshoot issues, improve Kine, protect the security of the app, and comply with legal obligations.

Do not send us media files, passwords, private keys, account tokens, or other sensitive information unless we specifically request it and you are comfortable sharing it.

## 5. Meta Developer Dashboard data

Meta may provide developers with aggregated or technical app information through the Meta Horizon Developer Dashboard, such as crash frequency, app version, device model, operating system version, crash type, and sampled stack traces. We may use this information to understand crashes, improve stability, and maintain Kine. We do not use this information to identify individual users.

Meta’s own processing of Meta Quest, Meta Horizon Store, device, purchase, crash, and platform data is governed by Meta’s policies, not this Kine Privacy Policy.

## 6. Internet access, media sources, and third-party services

Kine may connect to the internet, your local network, or third-party services when you choose to use features that require those connections. Examples include:

- Streaming a URL you enter.
- Connecting to a local network share or media server.
- Using UPnP, DLNA, SMB, NFS, WebDAV, HTTP, HTTPS, or similar media sources.
- Downloading subtitles, artwork, metadata, or add-ons.
- Using optional third-party add-ons, repositories, scrapers, metadata providers, or streaming services.

When you connect to a third-party service, that service may receive information necessary to provide the service, such as your IP address, request URL, search query, media title, account credentials, device/app information, or other data you provide to that service. Those third-party services are controlled by their own operators and are governed by their own privacy policies.

Kine does not control third-party add-ons, repositories, streaming services, media servers, subtitle providers, metadata providers, or network shares that you choose to use. Install add-ons only from sources you trust.

**Bundled third-party services or default providers:** None.

## 7. How we use data

We use data processed by Kine only for the following purposes:

- To provide media playback and media-library functionality.
- To connect to media sources, servers, streams, or services selected by you.
- To save your settings, playback progress, and preferences.
- To display metadata, artwork, subtitles, and library information.
- To support optional add-ons or integrations that you enable.
- To verify app entitlement where required for Meta Store distribution.
- To troubleshoot bugs, crashes, and support requests.
- To improve stability, compatibility, performance, and security.
- To respond to privacy, support, or security requests.
- To comply with applicable law.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your data for targeted advertising. We do not use your local media library or playback history to profile you.

## 8. Sharing of data

We do not share your local Kine media library, playback history, saved sources, saved credentials, or local logs with third parties unless you choose to send or disclose that information.

We may share limited information only in the following situations:

1. **At your direction.** For example, when you configure Kine to connect to a media server, network share, streaming service, metadata provider, subtitle provider, or add-on.
2. **With service providers.** For example, email hosting, issue tracking, website hosting, or support tools used to respond to your messages. These providers may process support information on our behalf.
3. **For legal or safety reasons.** If required by law, legal process, security obligations, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users, the app, or others.
4. **With Meta platform services.** To the extent required for Meta Horizon Store distribution, app entitlement, app operation, compliance, crash analytics, or Developer Dashboard functionality.

## 9. User choices and deletion

### Delete local Kine data

Most Kine data is stored locally on your Meta Quest device. You can delete local Kine data by using Kine’s own settings, where available, or by using the Meta Quest / Android app settings to clear Kine’s app data or uninstall Kine.

Deleting or clearing Kine app data may delete:

- Local media library database.
- Watch history and resume points.
- Cached thumbnails and artwork.
- App settings.
- Saved media sources.
- Saved local/network credentials.
- Local logs.
- Add-on settings and add-on data.

Deleting local Kine data from your device does not delete information held by third-party services, such as media servers, streaming providers, subtitle providers, metadata providers, or add-on operators. To delete data held by those services, contact the relevant third-party provider.

### Request deletion of data we hold

Kine does not operate user accounts and does not automatically collect your local media library or playback history on our servers.

If you have contacted us for support, submitted a bug report, sent logs, or otherwise provided information to us, you may request access, correction, or deletion by emailing:

**kine-app@outlook.com**

Use the subject line: **Kine Privacy Request**

We will not charge a fee for deletion requests. We may ask for information needed to verify and process the request. We will respond within a reasonable time and in accordance with applicable law. We may retain information where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution reasons.

## 10. Retention

Local Kine data remains on your device until you delete it, clear Kine’s app data, or uninstall Kine.

Support emails, bug reports, security reports, and privacy requests are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the request, maintain app security, improve Kine, and comply with legal obligations.

Aggregated or de-identified crash, performance, or support information may be retained for longer where it does not identify an individual user.

## 11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information we receive. Kine’s local data is stored in the app’s local storage area on your device. You should protect your Meta Quest device, Meta account, local network, media servers, and third-party service accounts with appropriate passwords and security settings.

Some network protocols or media sources may not be encrypted, depending on what you configure. For example, local network shares or HTTP streams may expose information to other devices on your network. Use trusted networks and trusted services.

No app, device, network, or transmission method is completely secure.

## 12. International processing

If you contact us, your support request may be processed in the United Kingdom and in other countries where our service providers operate. Privacy and data protection laws in those countries may differ from the laws where you live.

## 13. Children

Kine is not designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or under the age requiring parental consent in their jurisdiction. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us at **kine-app@outlook.com** and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.
